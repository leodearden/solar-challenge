## Per-hotspot findings

### 1. scenario-config (simulation-core)

**Diagnosis.** config.py reads 51 numeric values in three styles: raw splat into the domain dataclass, inline float() or int(), or the _number coercion table. None of them enforces the rule that a number is finite, never a bool, whole where it is a count, and refused with its key path (fk-649851e5fc38). The domain constructors that receive the splatted values check bounds only. They raise without the block path the reader holds, and some errors escape as TypeError (fk-e2aaf2bfe651). Each component's fields and defaults are written up to four times: in the dataclass, the *DistributionConfig, its parser's data.get fallbacks, and generate_homes_from_distribution (fk-148991ab9439). scenario_writer.py is a second grammar kept in step by hand, and it already loses PVConfig.system_losses on a round trip (fk-2b16cc8bcf46). Which kind of document a file is gets decided by key-presence tests in five or more places. `home run` edits the raw dict before parsing, so a flat 9 kW home runs as 4 kW (fk-2264bc150fa3). run_parameter_sweep and the _modify_* copy-constructors have no production caller, yet live tasks 237 and 320 are repairing them (fk-59fdc80fdded). ConfigurationError and the two battery-domain config types sit in the top module. finance, gridservices and battery therefore reach back with eleven deferred imports and TYPE_CHECKING imports (fk-4435376bedb3). The heuristic-14 measurement of config.py names the fleet-distribution grammar and sampler as the first partition. It can move only after the error type and the number primitives sit below it (fk-2dc9d1cd2c06). The commits arrive as TDD triples, but the recurrence is real: one defect class, fixed one reader at a time.

### 3. web-jobs-runs (web-dashboard)

**Diagnosis.** A job's state is four separately written facts: JobState.status, _TrackedJob.finished_at, the jobs row and the runs row. No legal-transition table governs them. A job becomes terminal in _run_job's try, not at the executor boundary, so an exception raised before that try leaves a finished job marked running (fk-484952247bb6).
