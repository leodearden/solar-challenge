`board_report.generate_finance_report` now takes `sections: Mapping[OwnUseBasis, AssumptionSection]`, then the keywords `scenario_name`, `flex_band`, `flex_band_name` and `grid_services_at_events`. This replaces its `bill_physics` and `bill_spreadsheet` positionals and its `economics` and `cost_recovery` keywords. The new frozen dataclass `board_report.AssumptionSection` holds one own-use mode's figures, every one computed under its own `finance: FinanceConfig`: its `bill: BillDistribution` and optional `projection`, `economics` and `cost_recovery`. The new `board_report.ASSUMPTION_LABELS` maps `OwnUseBasis.SIMULATED` to `"Physics"` and `OwnUseBasis.OVERRIDE` to `"Spreadsheet"`. Every table of the report has one value column per section, in `OwnUseBasis` order, headed by that label and, for an override, the self-consumption it sets, e.g. `Spreadsheet (70% self-consumption)`; no heading names a mode. The report raises `ValueError`, naming the values, in this order: for an empty mapping; for a key that is not `own_use_basis` of its section's finance; for sections that carry different computations of `projection`, `economics` and `cost_recovery`; and for bills annualised from different windows. `finance.own_use_basis(finance)`, the one rule of a `FinanceConfig`'s own-use basis, and `finance.with_own_use_basis(finance, basis)`, which puts a `FinanceConfig` on a basis and changes no other field, are new public names; `finance` has no `__all__`, so neither is in one. Consumers that call `generate_finance_report` build one `AssumptionSection` per mode when they re-pin, its finance from `with_own_use_basis`, and pass them keyed by `own_use_basis(section.finance)`.
