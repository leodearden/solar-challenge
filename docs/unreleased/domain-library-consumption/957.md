Every scenario document form, a bare scenario, a `scenario:` block or each `scenarios:`
entry, whose homes come from `home:`, `homes:` or `fleet_distribution:`, is read by one
grammar, through the new `config.parse_scenario(document, *, block_path="")` and
`config.load_scenario(path)`. `name:` and `period:` are optional: an absent name reads as
`""`, an absent or null period as `timebase.DEFAULT_FIRST_DAY` to `DEFAULT_LAST_DAY` on the
location's clock. A top-level key outside the grammar is refused, and exactly one homes
source is required. `load_fleet_config`, `parse_fleet_config`, `load_home_config` and
`parse_sweep_fleet_configs` now give their homes the document's top-level `tariff:`, a
home's own differing tariff being refused, and its `seg:` rate, as a `SEGTariff` with no
name. `load_fleet_config` reads a `home:` document as a one-home fleet, `load_home_config`
refuses a fleet document, and a flat home file (top-level `pv:`, `battery:`, `load:`,
`heat_pump:` or `ev:`) or a top-level `dispatch_strategy` is refused, naming `home:` or the
battery's key. `ScenarioConfig` gains `flex_band: GridServicesBand | None = None`, and
`fleet run` now books SEG export revenue on a file with `seg:`. A consumer re-pinning wraps
a flat home file in `home:`, renames `tariff_config:` to `tariff:`, drops any top-level key
the grammar refuses, and stops threading the file's SEG rate onto loaded homes by hand.
