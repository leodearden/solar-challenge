The fleet grammar's types read their numbers when they are built, through
`solar_challenge.number_fields`, and raise `ConfigurationError` naming the
field and the value: `WeightedDiscreteDistribution`'s `weights`,
`NormalDistribution`'s `mean`, `std`, `min` and `max`, `UniformDistribution`'s
`min` and `max`, `ShuffledPoolDistribution`'s `counts`,
`ProportionalDistribution`'s `multiplier` and `offset`, `SweepSpec`'s `min`,
`max` and `steps`, and `FleetDistributionConfig`'s `n_homes` and `seed`. They
hold Python floats and ints, and a `weighted_discrete`'s or `shuffled_pool`'s
`values` as a tuple of them as given. So a fractional `n_homes` or pool count
is refused where it was truncated (`n_homes: 2.7` ran 2 homes), and so is a
bool or fractional seed. A scalar or `fixed` number in a `fleet_distribution:`
block is refused as the block is read, naming its path, when it is a bool, text
or not finite: `capacity_kw: true` was a 1.0 kW home. A bare number in a
`ComponentDistribution` built in code reaches the home's type as given, so a
`True` is refused there rather than read as 1.0. A refusal
`FleetDistributionConfig` makes while `parse_fleet_distribution_config` builds
it now starts `fleet_distribution: `. EV arrival and departure hours drawn from
a `normal`, `uniform` or `proportional_to` are rounded to the nearest whole
hour, a half rounding up, not truncated, and a listed or fixed fractional EV
hour is refused by `EVConfig`, as an occupant count is. So seeded EV fleets
change: `scenarios/bristol-with-evs.yaml`'s 30 EV homes' mean arrival moves
from 17.433 to 17.933 and their mean departure from 6.533 to 7.2.
`solar_challenge.scenario_blocks.float_or_none`, which the task 947 line
lists, is deleted. Consumers give `n_homes`, seeds and pool counts as whole
numbers and fleet numbers as numbers, not text, expect a seeded EV fleet's
hours to move, and regenerate any stored expectation of one, when they re-pin.
