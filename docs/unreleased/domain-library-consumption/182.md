A tariff whose periods share one rate has no cheap period, so time-of-use
dispatch on it is self-consumption. The 0.5.0 tag classifies a minute as cheap
when its rate is at or below the mean of the period rates, so every minute of a
flat tariff such as `TariffConfig.flat_rate(0.25)` or
`economy_7(off_peak_rate=0.25, peak_rate=0.25)` is a cheap minute there;
`simulate_timestep_tou` holds the battery through all of them and imports the
whole shortfall. Task 182 changed that on `main`, and task 838 carries it on: a
flat tariff's `cheap_minutes_of_day` is empty and `is_cheap_at` is `False` at
every minute. A 2 kW shortfall for an hour against a fresh default 5 kWh
battery (2.5 kWh stored) at 03:00 on a flat 0.25 £/kWh tariff discharges 0 kWh
and imports 2.0 kWh (£0.50) on the tag, and discharges 1.95 kWh and imports
0.05 kWh (£0.0125) now, the result `simulate_timestep` returns with no tariff.
A home goes the same way under `simulate_home`, and so under `FleetRunner` and
`home run`. On the tag, a `HomeConfig` whose `dispatch_strategy` is
`'tou_optimized'` on a flat tariff, with a battery that names no strategy of
its own, runs through `simulate_timestep_tou` (task 904's note), so its battery
never discharges over the whole run and only charges from surplus PV; it now
discharges as self-consumption does. The `GridChargeContext` that
`simulate_timestep` builds from a tariff for a grid-charging battery changes
with it: its `is_cheap_period` is `True` at every minute of a flat tariff on
the tag and `False` at every minute now. Yet no built-in dispatch charged from
the grid on a flat tariff on the tag: `compute_grid_charge_power_kw` refused a
charge whose peak rate does not cover the round-trip loss, and a flat tariff's
peak rate, being its current rate, never covers it. So grid-charge figures do
not move. What does change is the `is_cheap_period` that a consumer's own
`DispatchStrategy` receives in `grid_charge_ctx`. Tariffs with a rate spread
classify as before task 182, apart from what task 838's note describes.
Consumers who run time-of-use dispatch on a flat tariff, through
`simulate_timestep_tou` or through `simulate_home` (and so `FleetRunner` and
`home run`) with a `HomeConfig` whose `dispatch_strategy` is `'tou_optimized'`,
re-baseline the figures built on that dispatch when they re-pin. Consumers
whose own `DispatchStrategy` reads `grid_charge_ctx.is_cheap_period` on a flat
tariff check what it does now that the flag is `False`. Other consumers need no
change.
