`FleetProjection`, which `project_fleet` and `run_cost_recovery` return, gains a third field, `grid_services_income_gbp`: (age, £/yr) for every age the projection simulated, its seeds and every bisection trial, ascending, each the grid-services income the scenario's pricing booked on that age's own simulated fleet; its first entry is age 0 (year 1) and its last the curve's last year. Construction refuses, with `ValueError` naming the series, one that is empty, not strictly ascending, not starting at age 0, not ending at the curve's last year, missing one of the curve's `sampled_ages`, or holding an income that is negative, NaN or infinite. `solar_challenge.board_report` gains `GridServicesIncome`, what a pricing that books grid services (`FlatGridServices` or `GridServicesEventsConfig`, never `NoGridServices`) books on the bill run's fleet: its income, battery homes and Σ battery discharge kW, and under events pricing its `GridServicesAtEvents` breakdown, refusing with `ValueError` a figure its pricing could not have printed; and `FlexBlock`, the flexibility-value block's `GridServicesBand`, holding the band a word names and refusing any other value with `ConfigurationError` naming `FlexBlock.band`, whose consulting figures `flex.resolve_flex_band` reads from `flex.FLEX_VALUE_BANDS`. `generate_finance_report`'s keywords `grid_services` and `flex` replace `flex_band`, `flex_band_name` and `grid_services_at_events`: given a `GridServicesIncome` it renders a Grid Services Income section after Project Economics, with the pricing's model line, each mode's booked income at year 1 and year N when the sections carry projections, and this window's fleet table, which now holds the capacity-at-events rows the flexibility-value block used to carry; the block's grid-services row reads the booked income, or the band's consulting figure labelled not booked. Neither report module is frozen, and nothing in `__all__` changes. Consumers that call `generate_finance_report` build a `GridServicesIncome` from their pricing value on the bill run's fleet and a `FlexBlock` from their band when they re-pin; consumers that construct neither can read the projection's booked income from the new field.
