`gridservices.GridServicesAtEvents` gains a last field, with no default,
`per_window_battery_export_kwh_per_event`: per event window, in window order, the
battery export an event holds (kWh), the battery homes' `battery_export` over the
window's rows on each event day, summed over homes and averaged over the event
days; a home whose results hold no `battery_export` adds 0, and a window with no
event day gives 0.0. It holds one entry per event window, each a number of at
least 0, refused as the other per-window fields are.
`compute_grid_services_at_events` fills it. The two displaced-self-use fields,
`per_window_displaced_self_use_kwh_per_event` and
`per_window_displaced_import_gbp_per_year`, are now reported together or not at
all: each holds one entry per event window as before, or both are `()`, the
breakdown's one "not reported", which construction now accepts; one of them `()`
while the other holds entries is still refused, now naming both fields and their
values. The 0.6.0 tag has neither the field nor the empty pair.

`compute_grid_services_at_events` and `GridServicesEventsConfig.annual_income_gbp`
read the configuration's `participation`. Under `observed`, the default, they
price the spare capacity as before, figure for figure, and refuse, with a
`ConfigurationError`, a battery home whose `BatteryConfig.event_discharge` holds
any window. Under `dispatched` they refuse a battery home whose `event_discharge`
is not `gridservices.enrolled_event_windows(cfg, its results' window)`. In both
directions the message names the participation, the home's position and name,
and the windows expected and found, as ISO dates at their hours. A dispatched
fleet is then priced, per event window, from the mean over the window's drawn
days of the delivery metered on each. Under the `capacity` baseline that is the
battery homes' discharge energy in the window over its `event_hours`. Under the
`operational` baseline it is how far the fleet's mean net import in the window
falls below its mean over the `gridservices.OPERATIONAL_BASELINE_DAYS` (10)
eligible days with no event of any window nearest before the day, completed
with the nearest after it, and at least 0. A window that draws no day of the
simulated days prices £0 and warns as a window the index never enters does.
Under the operational baseline, a window whose every eligible simulated day is
drawn is refused with a `ConfigurationError` naming the window. Dispatched
pricing reads each battery home's `SimulationResults.window`, so it refuses an
index that property refuses; observed `annual_income_gbp` still reads no step
length. A dispatched breakdown leaves both displaced-self-use fields `()`.

`gridservices.enrolled_event_windows(events, period)` returns the event windows
a dispatched fleet's battery homes are enrolled in over a `SimulationWindow`:
one `dispatch.EventDischargeWindow` per window of `events` that draws a day of
the period, in window order, its days `drawn_event_days` over the period's
dates with the events' seed at the window's position, in the window's hours.
`ScenarioConfig.simulated_homes` enrols with it, so the homes it returns are
unchanged. `gridservices.OPERATIONAL_BASELINE_DAYS` is `10`. Neither joins
`solar_challenge.__all__`.

`board_report`, outside the frozen surface, renders the participation. The
Grid Services Income section's model line ends `, <participation>
participation`. Its events table gains a `Battery export per event` row after
the 90% row. Under `dispatched` the delivery rows read `Per-event metered
delivery`, `Delivery on 50% of event days` and `Delivery on 90% of event days`,
the two displaced rows read `not reported`, and the note under the table says
the bills include the import the events caused. `GridServicesIncome` refuses,
with a `ValueError` naming the participation and both displaced fields, an
events breakdown that reports displaced self-use under dispatched pricing or
leaves it empty under observed pricing.

On re-pin, a consumer that builds a `GridServicesAtEvents` passes
`per_window_battery_export_kwh_per_event`. One that prices a dispatched fleet it
built itself enrols each battery home with
`dataclasses.replace(battery, event_discharge=enrolled_event_windows(cfg, results.window))`,
as `ScenarioConfig.simulated_homes` does. One that reads the finance report's
model line reads its new participation suffix.
