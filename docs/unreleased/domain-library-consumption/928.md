`YearPoint`, `MultiYearCurve`, `ProjectEconomics`, `MAX_NODES`, `project_multi_year`, `project_economics` and `spreadsheet_revenue_curve` move from `solar_challenge.finance` to the new module `solar_challenge.projection`, whose import loads pvlib. `project_economics(curve, fleet, finance)` takes a `ScenarioFleet` where the 0.5.0 tag takes the `ScenarioConfig`: `scenario_fleet(scenario)` gives it, the scenario's `simulated_homes()` over its `simulation_bounds()`. `solar_challenge.projection` also defines `ScenarioFleet`, whose construction raises `ValueError` naming `homes` when there are none, and `scenario_fleet`. `solar_challenge.finance` gains `one_window(summaries)`, outside `__all__`, the one window summaries billed or projected together cover, raising `ValueError` naming the first summary over another window. The frozen `solar_challenge.solve_cost_recovery_rate` keeps its form and its result, and still resolves to `solar_challenge.finance`, whose `solve_cost_recovery_rate` calls `solar_challenge.projection`'s. `import solar_challenge.finance` still loads neither pvlib nor `solar_challenge.fleet`. The platform (solar-challenge-platform 45ecca2) imports none of the moved names: it imports only the frozen `solar_challenge.solve_cost_recovery_rate`. When they re-pin, consumers import the moved names from `solar_challenge.projection` and pass `project_economics` `scenario_fleet(scenario)`.
