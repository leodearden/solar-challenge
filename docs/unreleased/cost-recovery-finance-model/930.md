`solar_challenge.projection` gains `CostRecoveryRun(projection, economics, solution)`, `run_cost_recovery(fleet, finance, *, simulate)` and `fewest_simulated_ages(asset_life_years)` (§5). `run_cost_recovery` projects a fleet once at the configured own-use rate, and that one projection gives its economics, its solve and the solve's outlay. `solve_cost_recovery_rate` is that run's solution: it bills its outlay on the projection's age-0 summaries, the same fleet it used to simulate a second time as `cr4-solve-age0`. So the solve now asks its simulator for 5 fleets at a 25-year life instead of 6, and its projection's warnings name the solve's caller. `optimize.run_sweep` makes one projection per config: 5 simulations at a 25-year life where main before this change asks 12, and 3 at a 3-year life where it asks 8. `ConfigResult` gains `baseline_own_use_rate_pence_per_kwh`, the configured rate its baseline pair is priced at. Its `baseline_outlay_gbp` now bills the new-install age-0 fleet with the scenario's SEG. The figure moves for scenarios whose batteries carry a configured age or SOH, because their age-0 battery is now at SOH 1.0: the 0.5.0 tag billed, for example, the calendar 0.88 of a 6-year battery. That figure is one axis of the board's Pareto front. `generate_config_ranking_report`'s second table is now headed '## Configured-Rate Trade-Off' and gains a 'Configured Rate (p/kWh)' column; the 0.5.0 tag prints '## Fixed-15p Trade-Off'. `finance run --project --cost-recovery` now asks for 6 simulations where main before this change asks 12, or 5 where the age-0 fleet is the bill run's (9 before). `optimize configs`' large-sweep warning counts at least `fewest_simulated_ages` per config's projection. The counts were measured with a constant stand-in simulator, on main at this task's merge base and on this change. When they re-pin, board readers re-check baseline outlays and the Pareto front on aged-battery scenarios. Report parsers read the new heading and column. Code that constructs `ConfigResult` passes `baseline_own_use_rate_pence_per_kwh`. A consumer that wants the projection, the economics and the solve from one projection calls `run_cost_recovery`.
