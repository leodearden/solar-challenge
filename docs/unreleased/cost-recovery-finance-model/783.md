`generate_finance_report` (`finance run`) and `generate_config_ranking_report` (`optimize configs`) print, under their first heading, "*Yearly figures are extrapolated ×<annualisation_scale, 3 dp> from a <days>-day window (<first day> to <last day>), not whole calendar years.*" when their bills' `annualised_from` window runs other than from 1 January to 31 December (§4, *Annual basis*; §9). The notice replaces the short-period warnings that task 782's note retires. A distribution with no `annualised_from`, which only a hand-built one has, renders none, and neither does a ranking with no feasible config. The CLI's status lines and `optimize configs`' large-sweep warning count the run's calendar days: a run that starts on GMT and ends on BST, such as 2024-03-31 to 2024-04-01, counts 2 days where the 0.5.0 tag counts 1. When they re-pin, consumers that parse either report expect the notice paragraph after its first heading.
