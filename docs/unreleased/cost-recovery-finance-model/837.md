Capacity-at-events income in `project_multi_year` and `finance run` is priced at the mean over event days of the fleet's daily firm spare capacity, under the events configuration's `delivery_baseline`, `operational` by default (§4, §8.1); the 0.5.0 tag takes the minimum over every in-window timestep of the season, so one minute at a battery's SOC floor zeroes that home for the whole season there. The finance report's capacity-at-events table adds the capacity available on 50% and on 90% of event days and the evening self-use an event displaces per event, shown for a single event window and as — for several, and that self-use's value per year, summed over the windows, £0 for a home simulated without a tariff; the income is gross of it (§8.1). Re-read any capacity-at-events figure taken from the 0.5.0 tag; flat and no-grid-services figures are unchanged.
