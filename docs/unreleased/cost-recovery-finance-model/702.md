`YearPoint.fleet_self_consumption_kwh`, the fleet's billed own-use (§4) and the cost-recovery solve's rate base (§5), is renamed `fleet_billed_own_use_kwh`; the 0.5.0 tag has the old name. `project_multi_year` also no longer floors a year's `fleet_revenue_gbp` at £0, so a `simulate` whose fleet earns a negative revenue projects, and the solve prices, that loss; the 0.5.0 tag projects it as £0. Its adaptive refinement measures each metric's interpolation error against the magnitude of that metric's age-0 value, so a fleet whose revenue is negative at age 0 and varies with age converges as the fleet earning the mirror revenue does; the 0.5.0 tag divides that fleet's revenue error by 1e-9, so its bisection typically runs to `MAX_NODES` and reports a meaningless `interp_error_estimate`. Fleets `simulate_fleet` builds are unaffected: their own-use, SEG and grid-services income is never negative.
