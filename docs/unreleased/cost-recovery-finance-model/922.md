Finance now prices each home's grid flows by how its simulated run was priced (`SummaryStatistics.pricing`, §3, §4), instead of inferring it from the pounds the run reported. A home on a £0 tariff is billed £0 of import with no warning, where the 0.5.0 tag billed it at the retail rate with the "no tariff configured" warning. With `self_consumption_override` set, a home that simulated no export earns the CBS its SEG at its own rate on the override's export: £49.20 on 1,200 kWh at 4.1 p, where the tag gives £0.00. A priced override home that simulated no import is billed the override's import at its tariff's time-weighted mean rate rather than at retail. Physics bills and projections do not move beyond float precision. When they re-pin, board readers re-check the bills of homes on a £0 tariff, the override-mode SEG revenue, and the solved own-use rates that read them.
