Each projection year's revenue between simulated ages (§4, *Per-year revenue*) is now the own-use rate × that year's billed own-use / 100 plus the year's income, SEG plus grid services, with own-use and income each interpolated by PCHIP through their values at the simulated ages; revenue itself is no longer interpolated. The projection's choice of which ages to simulate (§4, *Node selection*) now measures the income's interpolation error, not the revenue's, against its largest value at the seed ages, and the own-use's against its largest value there, so it reads no own-use rate. So the cost-recovery solve (§5) is exact: its shifted line is a re-projection at every rate, and its solved rate no longer moves with the configured `own_use_rate_pence_per_kwh`. The 0.5.0 tag (solar-challenge-v0.5.0) interpolates revenue itself: on task 720's nodes its shifted line was off a re-projection by up to £38.74 in a year, and on the same nodes its solved rate moves by 0.017 p/kWh across configured rates of 0, 15 and 23 p. Board figures move with it, where review-all S8 estimated per-year revenue between simulated ages by £41–150 a year and the solved rate by at most 0.01 p/kWh. Measured on `scenarios/bristol-phase1.yaml` as `finance run` projects it (100 homes, 15 p own-use rate, the committed Bristol TMY), the simulated ages (0, 6, 12, 24), the `interp_error_estimate` (0.686 %) and every year's own-use do not move, and a year's revenue moves by up to £94.71 (year 17, £33,239.53 to £33,334.24), £22.83 a year higher on the mean, which at that fleet's mean own-use moves its solved rate by about −0.014 p/kWh (derived from the mean, not solved). A fleet whose income is £0 at age 0, at a 0 p own-use rate, no longer simulates 12 ages and reports an `interp_error_estimate` of billions of percent. `solar_challenge.projection` gains `project_fleet(fleet, finance, *, simulate, error_target_pct=1.0)`, the projection of a `ScenarioFleet`, returning a frozen `FleetProjection` whose `curve` is `project_multi_year`'s and whose `age0_summaries` are each home's summary of the fleet simulated at age 0, in fleet order. When they re-pin, board readers re-check projections and solved rates; a library consumer that wants the age-0 summaries reads `project_fleet(...).age0_summaries`.
