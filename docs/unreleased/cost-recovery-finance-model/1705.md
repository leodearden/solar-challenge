A dispatched fleet's grid-services events income
(`grid_services_model.participation: dispatched`) is the delivery metered from
its enrolled run on each drawn event day, booked as the mean over the drawn days
at §4's rates (§8.1, *Metered delivery (dispatched)*). Under the capacity
baseline a day's delivery is the battery homes' discharge energy in the window
over the event's hours. Under the operational baseline it is how far the fleet's
mean net import in the window falls below its mean over the ten nearest eligible
days with no event, and at least 0. An observed fleet's income is unchanged.
Pricing refuses a fleet whose battery homes carry other event windows than its
participation gives them, naming the participation and the days expected and
found. A dispatched breakdown reports no displaced self-use, since the same
run's bills hold the import the events caused. Both participations report the
battery export per event. The finance report's model line names the
participation, and its table adds a battery-export row. Under dispatched, the
table labels the delivery rows as metered, reads the displaced rows as not
reported, and notes that the bills include the events' import. The 0.6.0 tag
has no dispatched participation. A reader of a dispatched scenario's report
reads its income as metered delivery. No figure of an observed scenario moves.
