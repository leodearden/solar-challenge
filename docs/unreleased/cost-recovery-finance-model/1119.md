`project_multi_year`, and so the cost-recovery solve, raises `ValueError` when its `simulate` returns, for an age t, a fleet that is not §4's F_t, the scenario's homes at age t: one whose `home_configs` hold another number of homes, or do not carry the scenario's batteries in order, state of health aside. Grid-services income is priced from F_t's batteries (§4), so such a fleet would misprice it, a flat rate at £0 for a fleet returned without batteries. The message names the age's fleet, `proj-age-<t>`, then both numbers of homes, or the first home whose batteries differ and both batteries; the 0.5.0 tag accepts such a fleet. When they re-pin, consumers whose injected `simulate` builds its own results return the batteries of the homes it is asked to simulate, as `FleetRunner.simulate` does.
